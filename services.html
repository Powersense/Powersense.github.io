Services

Studies, delivered

The same engine we license, applied by the engineer who built it. A limited number of engagements are taken each quarter so software development stays first.

Congestion & deliverability screens

Contingency-based screening of candidate points of interconnection or portfolio positions: binding constraints, transfer headroom, and worst-case limits, delivered as a reviewable report plus the underlying scripts.

Shadow restudies

Independent re-runs of queued-project study assumptions as cluster compositions shift — quantify how withdrawals and topology changes move your upgrade exposure before the official restudy lands.

Large-load feasibility scans

ERCOT-focused screening for data center and large-load interconnection: contingency and transfer-limit analysis of candidate sites under current study practice.

How engagements work

  • Your data, your environment. Models are handled under your data agreements; deliverables include the scripts, so the work is repeatable without us.
  • Fixed scope, fixed fee. Screening engagements are quoted as defined deliverables, not open-ended hours.
  • A path to self-serve. Every engagement doubles as a PsFlow evaluation — teams that want to bring the workflow in house license the engine and keep the scripts.
